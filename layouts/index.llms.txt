## Who Nathan is a good fit for

- Series A and B companies in Healthcare Tech or FinTech, roughly 5–50 engineers
- Companies with no technology executive, or a founding engineer who has outgrown the role
- Teams where the technical vision is clear but delivery is not
- Companies raising, selling, or entering a regulated market for the first time
- Acquirers and investors needing technical due diligence

## Who Nathan is NOT a good fit for

- Pre-seed companies needing someone to write the first version of the product
- Engagements where the plan is already settled and only execution hands are wanted
- Full-time, hands-on-keyboard architecture roles at 40 hours a week
